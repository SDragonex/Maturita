## Modelový projev B1/B2

*Originální fiktivní příklad pro jazykový nácvik. Osobní údaje a zkušenosti nejsou údaje o autorovi ani o tobě; při vlastní odpovědi je nahraď.*

Health is an important part of everyday life, and it includes how we feel both physically and mentally. In this fictional example, I try to balance schoolwork with activities that I enjoy. I like walking with friends because we can talk and spend time away from our screens. I also try to keep a regular routine, although this can be difficult before exams.

When I feel stressed, I make a short list of tasks and discuss problems with someone I trust. If I feel unwell, I need to explain my symptoms clearly. For example, I can say that I have had a sore throat since Monday. I would ask a healthcare professional for advice instead of choosing treatment from a random social media post. It is also useful to know how to make an appointment in English. I might need to spell my name, describe the problem and confirm the time.

## Otázky a krátké vzorové odpovědi

1. **How can you describe a problem to a doctor?**\
   I can say where it hurts and how long I have felt unwell.

2. **What helps you manage school stress?**\
   For me, planning tasks and talking to someone make things easier.

3. **How do you arrange an appointment?**\
   I call the practice and ask when the next appointment is available.

4. **Where would you ask about treatment?**\
   I would ask a qualified healthcare professional about my situation.

5. **What if you do not understand an instruction?**\
   I would say: Could you explain that again in simpler words, please?

## Gramatika a časté chyby

**I have a headache**, ale **I feel ill**. Délka dosud trvajícího stavu: I have felt unwell **for two days / since Monday**. Healthy je přídavné jméno, health podstatné. Doctor’s je ordinace v obratu at the doctor’s. Tato stránka procvičuje jazyk; neurčuje diagnózu ani léčbu.
