## Modelový projev B1/B2

*Originální fiktivní příklad pro jazykový nácvik. Osobní údaje a zkušenosti nejsou údaje o autorovi ani o tobě; při vlastní odpovědi je nahraď.*

I would like to describe a flat in a quiet neighbourhood. In this example, I live with my family on the second floor of a small building. There are two bedrooms, a living room, a kitchen and a bathroom. My favourite place is my bedroom because I can study there without much noise. My desk is next to the window, and there are bookshelves above it.

Living in a flat has several advantages. Shops and public transport are nearby, and there is less maintenance than in a house. On the other hand, we do not have a private garden, and sometimes we can hear our neighbours. Everyone helps with household chores. I usually take out the rubbish and clean my room. In the future, I would like to rent a small flat near my workplace. For me, a convenient location is more important than a large living room.

## Otázky a krátké vzorové odpovědi

1. **Would you prefer a house or a flat?**\
   I would prefer a flat because it would be easier to maintain.

2. **What is your favourite room?**\
   The living room, because it is where everyone spends time together.

3. **What makes a good neighbourhood?**\
   Safe streets, useful services and reliable public transport make a big difference.

4. **Who does the housework?**\
   We share it; for example, I wash the dishes after dinner.

5. **What would you change at home?**\
   I would add more storage space, so the rooms would feel less crowded.

## Gramatika a časté chyby

Pro vybavení použij **there is / there are**: There is a desk; there are two chairs. Rozlišuj **in the kitchen**, **on the first floor** a **at home**. Furniture je nepočitatelné: some furniture, nikoli furnitures. Po would následuje základní tvar: I would live.

## Obrázek a dialog: mini úkol

Představ si obrázek malého městského bytu a domu se zahradou. Uveď tři rozdíly a pro studenta vyber jednu možnost s důvodem. V dialogu s pronajímatelem se zeptej na nájem, vybavení a dopravu: Is electricity included in the rent?
