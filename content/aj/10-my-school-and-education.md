## Osnova odpovědi

- Představ typ školy, předměty a běžný školní den bez vymýšlení konkrétních pravidel SŠTE.

## Modelový projev B1/B2

*Originální fiktivní příklad pro jazykový nácvik. Osobní údaje a zkušenosti nejsou údaje o autorovi ani o tobě; při vlastní odpovědi je nahraď.*

In this fictional example, I attend a secondary school with a technical focus. My timetable includes general subjects, such as languages, as well as subjects related to information technology. I enjoy practical assignments because I can see how an idea works in a real project. For example, creating a small application involves planning, programming and checking the result.

Theory is important too. It helps me understand why a solution works instead of simply copying instructions. When I prepare for an exam, I divide the material into topics and try to explain each one aloud. If I cannot explain something clearly, I return to it. Group work can be useful, provided that everyone has a role. A good teacher gives understandable feedback and encourages students to ask questions. After secondary school, I would consider further study or a junior job with opportunities to learn. Education does not end with the final school examination.

## Otázky a krátké vzorové odpovědi

1. **Which subject do you enjoy and why?**\
   I enjoy programming because I can build something and test my ideas.

2. **How do you prepare for an exam?**\
   I practise explaining topics and review the mistakes I make.

3. **What are the benefits of group work?**\
   Students can share ideas and learn to communicate about a common task.

4. **Would you prefer online or classroom lessons?**\
   I prefer classroom discussion, but online materials are useful for revision.

5. **What would you improve at a school?**\
   I would provide more opportunities to work on realistic projects.

## Gramatika a časté chyby

**Learn** znamená učit se, **teach** učit někoho. **Study at a school**, **attend school**, **take an exam** a **pass an exam** nejsou zaměnitelné: účast ještě nezaručuje úspěch. **I have been studying for three years** vyjadřuje trvající studium.
