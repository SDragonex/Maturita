## Modelový projev B1/B2

*Originální fiktivní příklad pro jazykový nácvik. Osobní údaje a zkušenosti nejsou údaje o autorovi ani o tobě; při vlastní odpovědi je nahraď.*

Each season offers different opportunities, and my plans often depend on the weather. In spring, I enjoy seeing trees turn green and spending more time outside. Summer is suitable for longer trips, although very hot days can make sightseeing less pleasant. Autumn is interesting because of the colours, but I usually carry an umbrella. In winter, short days and cold mornings change my daily routine.

In this fictional example, my favourite season is spring. I like mild weather because I can walk comfortably without heavy clothes. Before a weekend trip, I check the forecast and prepare an alternative indoor activity. Last weekend, we planned a picnic, but it started raining, so we visited a museum instead. Weather describes conditions at a particular time and place. Climate describes typical patterns over a much longer period. One rainy afternoon therefore does not tell us everything about a region. Being flexible helps me enjoy a trip even when the forecast changes.

## Otázky a krátké vzorové odpovědi

1. **Which season do you prefer?**\
   I prefer spring because the days become longer and the weather is often mild.

2. **What do you wear in heavy rain?**\
   I wear a waterproof jacket and suitable shoes.

3. **How can weather affect transport?**\
   Fog, snow or strong winds can cause delays and change travel plans.

4. **What would you do if a picnic was cancelled?**\
   I would suggest a museum or a board game afternoon indoors.

5. **Is weather the same as climate?**\
   No; weather is short-term, while climate concerns long-term patterns.

## Gramatika a časté chyby

Počasí má formální podmět **it**: It is windy; it is raining. Rain je podstatné jméno i sloveso, rainy přídavné: heavy rain / a rainy day. Podmínka: **If it rains, we will stay inside**, nikoli if it will rain.

## Obrázek a dialog: mini úkol

Porovnej obrázek slunečné pláže a zasněžené ulice: oblečení, viditelnost, činnosti a nálada. V dialogu změň plán výletu po předpovědi bouřek. Uveď konkrétní alternativu i důvod: How about visiting the gallery instead?
