## Modelový projev B1/B2

*Originální fiktivní příklad pro jazykový nácvik. Osobní zkušenosti nejsou údaje o autorovi ani o tobě; uprav je pro vlastní odpověď.*

Festivals and traditions can bring people together and help them remember important events. However, people celebrate in different ways, even within one country. Some traditions are religious, while others are mainly family or community activities. It is useful to describe examples without assuming that everybody follows them.

In Czechia, many families celebrate Christmas with a meal and presents on Christmas Eve. Easter customs may include decorated eggs, although not every family practises the same traditions. In the United States, Thanksgiving is associated with a shared meal and giving thanks. Halloween is often connected with costumes and activities for children. These celebrations have different backgrounds and should not be confused with each other.

For a fictional school celebration, I would suggest an international evening where students explain a tradition they know. Participation would be voluntary, and we would ask about dietary needs before planning the food. My favourite part would be learning why a tradition matters to someone. I think a celebration works best when people feel welcome rather than pressured to take part.

## Otázky a krátké vzorové odpovědi

1. **Does everyone celebrate the same way?**\
   No, traditions vary between families and communities.

2. **What is Christmas Eve?**\
   It is the day before Christmas Day, on 24 December.

3. **Why do traditions matter?**\
   They can connect people with family memories.

4. **How would you organise a celebration?**\
   I would agree on a budget, activities and responsibilities.
