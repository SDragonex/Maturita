## Modelový projev B1/B2

*Originální fiktivní příklad pro jazykový nácvik. Osobní údaje a zkušenosti nejsou údaje o autorovi ani o tobě; při vlastní odpovědi je nahraď.*

Choosing a future job involves more than deciding how much money I would like to earn. I also need to think about my interests, skills and the kind of environment where I could learn. In this fictional example, I would like to apply for a junior position in software development. I enjoy solving problems, but I know that teamwork and communication are important too.

My CV would include my education, relevant skills and a few projects. Instead of claiming to be an expert, I would explain what I actually built and what I learned. During an interview, I would prepare examples of solving a problem or working with other people. I would also ask about everyday responsibilities and support for new employees. I do not expect my first job to determine my entire career. Gaining experience and learning from feedback would help me make better decisions about the future.

## Otázky a krátké vzorové odpovědi

1. **What would you include in a student CV?**\
   I would include education, relevant skills and projects that I can explain.

2. **What is one of your strengths?**\
   I am patient; for example, I keep investigating a problem instead of guessing.

3. **What would you ask an interviewer?**\
   I would ask what a typical working day looks like.

4. **How could you gain experience?**\
   I could complete a project, volunteer or apply for a suitable placement.

5. **Is a high salary the only priority?**\
   No; learning opportunities and working conditions are important as well.

## Gramatika a časté chyby

**Apply for a job**, ale **work for a company**. Job je počitatelné, work a experience zpravidla ne: a job / some work / relevant experience. Budoucí plán: I am going to apply. Zdvořilé přání: I would like to learn, nikoli I would like learn.

## Obrázek a dialog: mini úkol

Sepiš čtyři pravdivé odrážky do vlastního CV a ke každé připrav důkaz nebo příklad. V modelovém pohovoru představ projekt během jedné minuty, přiznej jednu oblast k učení a polož zaměstnavateli dvě otázky. Nevkládej do CV smyšlené zkušenosti z projevu.
