## Modelový projev B1/B2

*Originální fiktivní příklad pro jazykový nácvik. Osobní údaje a zkušenosti nejsou údaje o autorovi ani o tobě; při vlastní odpovědi je nahraď.*

Shopping can be an ordinary daily task or a decision that requires careful comparison. In this fictional example, I buy groceries in a local shop because it is nearby. When I need something more expensive, such as headphones, I compare several options first. I look at the features, price and delivery conditions instead of choosing only the lowest price.

Online shopping is convenient because I can browse products at home. However, I cannot always judge the quality from a photograph. In a physical shop, I can ask questions and sometimes try the product. Services are also part of everyday shopping: people visit hairdressers, repair shops or delivery offices. Good service means clear communication and attention to the customer's problem. If a product does not work, I keep the receipt and contact the seller. I explain what happened calmly and ask about the available solution. Being polite can help both people understand the situation.

## Otázky a krátké vzorové odpovědi

1. **Do you prefer online shopping?**\
   For some products, yes, but I prefer trying clothes on in a shop.

2. **What do you compare before buying headphones?**\
   I compare comfort, useful features, price and the seller’s information.

3. **Why is a receipt useful?**\
   It provides information about the purchase when I contact the seller.

4. **How would you describe a faulty product?**\
   I would say: These headphones do not produce any sound.

5. **What makes good customer service?**\
   Listening carefully and explaining the next step make a positive difference.

## Gramatika a časté chyby

**How much does it cost?** versus **How much are these shoes?** Oblečení: try **on** a jacket. **Too expensive** znamená příliš drahé, **expensive enough** dost drahé. Bought je minulý čas od buy. Advice je nepočitatelné: some advice, nikoli an advice.

## Obrázek a dialog: mini úkol

V dialogu přines do obchodu nefunkční sluchátka. Uveď datum nákupu jako vymyšlený údaj, popiš závadu a zeptej se na možnosti řešení. Potom porovnej obrázek malého obchodu a nákupního centra z hlediska výběru, prostředí a služeb.
