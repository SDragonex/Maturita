## Modelový projev B1/B2

*Originální fiktivní příklad pro jazykový nácvik. Osobní zkušenosti nejsou údaje o autorovi ani o tobě; uprav je pro vlastní odpověď.*

The environment includes the air, water, land and living things around us. People depend on these systems, so protecting them also matters for our own lives. Common environmental problems include pollution, waste and the loss of natural habitats. Climate change is another important topic, but weather on one particular day does not describe the whole climate.

In daily life, I can avoid wasting food, repair useful objects and choose reusable products when they make sense. I can also think about how I travel. Walking or public transport may be practical for some journeys, while other situations require a different solution. Individual action is useful, but businesses and public institutions also influence how much energy and material we use.

In this model project, a school wants to reduce waste. I would first find out what is being thrown away and why. Then I would suggest one realistic change and measure the result. For example, clearer information might help students use recycling bins correctly. I would rather explain a practical solution than blame people without understanding their situation.

## Otázky a krátké vzorové odpovědi

1. **What is the difference between weather and climate?**\
   Weather describes current conditions; climate describes patterns over a long period.

2. **How could a school reduce waste?**\
   It could measure waste and prevent unnecessary purchases.

3. **Is recycling enough?**\
   No, avoiding waste and reusing products are also useful.

4. **Who should protect the environment?**\
   Individuals, businesses and public institutions all have a role.

## Gramatika a časté chyby

Environment je podstatné jméno, environmental přídavné. Less waste, ale fewer plastic bottles. Advice je nepočitatelné. Používej should pro doporučení, must pro silnou povinnost.
