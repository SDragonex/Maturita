## Modelový projev B1/B2

*Originální fiktivní příklad pro jazykový nácvik. Osobní zkušenosti nejsou údaje o autorovi ani o tobě; uprav je pro vlastní odpověď.*

Prague is the capital of Czechia. I would start a visit in the Old Town and walk across Charles Bridge towards Prague Castle. These places show different periods of the city’s history. However, I would also leave time for a quiet park because a sightseeing trip can be tiring. Before travelling, I would check opening hours and book accommodation near public transport.

Czechia offers much more than its capital. Visitors can explore historical towns, mountain areas and castles. For a weekend, I would choose one region instead of trying to see the whole country. Travelling by train can be convenient, but a car may be useful for a remote village. In this fictional trip, a friend and I have a limited budget, so we compare transport and accommodation costs. If the weather is bad, we will visit a museum. I would recommend comfortable shoes and a realistic plan with time for breaks.

## Otázky a krátké vzorové odpovědi

1. **Which place would you show a visitor?**\
   I would show them the Old Town because it is easy to explore on foot.

2. **Would you travel by car or train?**\
   I would take the train for a city trip, but a car for a remote area.

3. **What can go wrong on a trip?**\
   Transport can be delayed, so I would leave extra time.

4. **How would you save money?**\
   I would compare accommodation and avoid unnecessary taxi journeys.

## Gramatika a časté chyby
