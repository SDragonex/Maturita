## Modelový projev B1/B2

*Originální fiktivní příklad pro jazykový nácvik. Osobní údaje a zkušenosti nejsou údaje o autorovi ani o tobě; při vlastní odpovědi je nahraď.*

On a typical school day in this example, I get up at half past six. First, I have a shower and get dressed. Then I prepare breakfast and check that I have everything for school. I usually travel by bus, so I leave home early enough to avoid rushing. During the journey, I sometimes listen to music.

After lessons, I have lunch and return home. I prefer to take a short break before starting my homework. If an assignment is difficult, I divide it into smaller tasks. Later, I help with a few chores and spend some time on my hobbies. In the evening, I prepare my bag for the next day. My weekend routine is more flexible: I get up later and meet friends. I would like to spend less time checking my phone because it interrupts my work. A useful routine should help me manage my time without making every day exactly the same.

## Otázky a krátké vzorové odpovědi

1. **What do you do first in the morning?**\
   I get up and open the window before preparing breakfast.

2. **How do you organise homework?**\
   I start with the most urgent task and take short breaks.

3. **What is different at weekends?**\
   I have more time for friends and do not follow a school timetable.

4. **Are you usually in a hurry?**\
   Sometimes, especially when I cannot find something before leaving home.

5. **What habit would you like to change?**\
   I would like to prepare my clothes the night before.

## Gramatika a časté chyby

Zvyky vyjadřuj **present simple**: I leave at seven; she leaves at seven. Frekvence stojí obvykle před hlavním slovesem: I often study; ale I am often tired. **At seven**, **on Monday**, **in the morning**. Homework je nepočitatelné; správně do homework, nikoli make homeworks.

## Obrázek a dialog: mini úkol

Porovnej obrázek člověka, který spěchá na autobus, s člověkem pracujícím doma. Rozliš zvyk a aktuální děj: He usually travels; he is running now. V dialogu domluv čas společného učení a reaguj na překážku: I cannot meet at four because I have a lesson.
