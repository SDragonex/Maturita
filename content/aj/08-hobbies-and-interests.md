## Osnova odpovědi

- Představ tvořivé, pohybové a odpočinkové zájmy; vysvětli osobní motivaci.
- Popiš jeden koníček tak, aby mu rozuměl začátečník, včetně vybavení a času.
- Porovnej samostatnou a společnou aktivitu a vztah mezi koníčkem a povoláním.

## Modelový projev B1/B2

*Originální fiktivní příklad pro jazykový nácvik. Osobní údaje a zkušenosti nejsou údaje o autorovi ani o tobě; při vlastní odpovědi je nahraď.*

Hobbies give people an opportunity to do something because they enjoy it, rather than because they have to. In this fictional example, I am interested in photography and board games. Photography encourages me to notice small details during ordinary walks. I started with a phone camera, so I did not need expensive equipment. I enjoy comparing pictures and thinking about how light changes a scene.

Board games are different because they bring people together. Before a game, someone needs to explain the rules, and new players may need a little help. I prefer friendly competition to arguments about winning. Some hobbies can develop useful professional skills, such as patience, communication or planning. However, I do not think every free-time activity must become a job. Sometimes the main purpose is simply to relax. In the future, I would like to try a creative workshop where I could learn something new and meet people with similar interests.

## Otázky a krátké vzorové odpovědi

1. **How did you start your hobby?**\
   A friend showed me the basics, and I practised with simple equipment.

2. **Do you prefer hobbies alone or with others?**\
   It depends: I like quiet activities alone and games with friends.

3. **Can an inexpensive hobby be interesting?**\
   Certainly; drawing or walking can be enjoyable without a large budget.

4. **What would you recommend to a beginner?**\
   I would suggest starting with a small project and enjoying the process.

5. **Should hobbies always be useful?**\
   No; having fun and relaxing are valuable reasons too.

## Gramatika a časté chyby

Po **enjoy** a po předložce následuje -ing: I enjoy drawing; I am interested in learning. Rozliš **boring** (nudný) a **bored** (znuděný). **Spend time doing something**: I spend an hour practising, nikoli spend an hour to practise.
