## Modelový projev B1/B2

*Originální fiktivní příklad pro jazykový nácvik. Osobní údaje a zkušenosti nejsou údaje o autorovi ani o tobě; při vlastní odpovědi je nahraď.*

People can enjoy sport in many different ways. Some prefer competing in a team, while others like individual activities such as running or cycling. In this fictional example, I play basketball with friends once a week. We use a local outdoor court, and we do not take the result too seriously. The best part is working together and improving our skills.

Basketball requires a ball, suitable shoes and a place with baskets. Players need to pass accurately and pay attention to the other team. I sometimes watch professional matches, but I prefer playing myself. Individual sports have advantages too: you can often choose your own timetable and practise at your own pace. However, some activities require expensive equipment. I think schools and towns should provide affordable opportunities for beginners. Sport should be enjoyable, and people should not feel embarrassed simply because they are less experienced than their friends.

## Otázky a krátké vzorové odpovědi

1. **Do you prefer playing or watching sport?**\
   I prefer playing because I enjoy being involved in the activity.

2. **What makes a good teammate?**\
   A good teammate communicates, follows the rules and supports others.

3. **Which sport would you like to try?**\
   I would like to try badminton because it looks fast and enjoyable.

4. **Is winning always important?**\
   No; learning a skill and enjoying the match can matter more.

5. **What can make sport expensive?**\
   Equipment, travel and membership fees can make it difficult to afford.

## Gramatika a časté chyby

Kolokace: **play basketball**, **go swimming**, **do gymnastics**. Rozliš **win a match** a **beat an opponent**. Equipment je nepočitatelné: some equipment. Minulý čas: win–won, lose–lost. Pro porovnání: Team sports can be more social than individual activities.
