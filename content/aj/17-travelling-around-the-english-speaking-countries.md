## Modelový projev B1/B2

*Originální fiktivní příklad pro jazykový nácvik. Osobní zkušenosti nejsou údaje o autorovi ani o tobě; uprav je pro vlastní odpověď.*

English is used in several countries, but these places have different histories and cultures. For example, the United Kingdom consists of England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. The Republic of Ireland is a separate country. The United States, Canada, Australia and New Zealand are other possible destinations for an English-speaking trip.

For this fictional holiday, I would choose the United Kingdom because I would like to practise English and visit museums. I would spend a few days in London and then travel to another region. Staying in one place longer would give me more time to explore it. I would compare prices, check travel documents and read current entry rules on an official website before booking.

Travelling abroad can improve language skills because you need to ask questions, understand announcements and solve everyday problems. Still, nobody needs perfect English to start a conversation. If I did not understand something, I would politely ask the speaker to repeat it or explain it in another way.

## Otázky a krátké vzorové odpovědi

1. **Why would you go abroad?**\
   I would like to practise English in everyday situations.

2. **Is England the same as the UK?**\
   No, England is one part of the United Kingdom.

3. **What would you check before booking?**\
   I would check official entry rules, costs and insurance.

4. **What if you cannot understand a speaker?**\
   I would say: Could you speak more slowly, please?

## Gramatika a časté chyby

Člen the: the United Kingdom, the United States. Bez členu: Canada, Australia. Rozliš go abroad a go to another country. Podmínková věta: If I had more time, I would travel further.

## Obrázek a dialog: mini úkol

Porovnej městskou dovolenou s pobytem v přírodě. V hotelu požádej o jiný pokoj a zdvořile vysvětli problém. Údaje o vízech jsou proměnlivé; uč se postup jejich ověření, ne univerzální odpověď.
