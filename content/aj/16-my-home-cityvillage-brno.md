## Modelový projev B1/B2

*Originální fiktivní příklad pro jazykový nácvik. Osobní zkušenosti nejsou údaje o autorovi ani o tobě; uprav je pro vlastní odpověď.*

For this speaking exercise, I will describe Brno as a place where someone could live and study. Brno is a city in Moravia, in the eastern part of Czechia. It has universities, offices, shops and many opportunities to spend free time. Visitors can explore the city centre, Špilberk Castle and the Cathedral of Saints Peter and Paul.

One advantage of living in a city is public transport. You do not need a car for every journey, and services are usually nearby. On the other hand, traffic and noise can make some neighbourhoods less comfortable. A village may offer more space and a quieter environment, but commuting can take longer. In my ideal neighbourhood, there would be a library, a park and a reliable connection to school. If I were showing a friend around, I would combine a short walk in the centre with a relaxed afternoon outdoors. I would ask about their interests before choosing the route.

## Otázky a krátké vzorové odpovědi

1. **What do you like about city life?**\
   I like having useful services within walking distance.

2. **What would you improve?**\
   I would improve safe cycling routes.

3. **Would you live in a village?**\
   Possibly, if I could reach my workplace easily.

4. **How would you show a visitor around?**\
   I would ask what they enjoy and choose a short route.
