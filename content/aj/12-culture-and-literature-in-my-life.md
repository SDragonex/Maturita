## Modelový projev B1/B2

*Originální fiktivní příklad pro jazykový nácvik. Osobní údaje a zkušenosti nejsou údaje o autorovi ani o tobě; při vlastní odpovědi je nahraď.*

Culture gives us different ways to share ideas and understand other people's experiences. It includes books, films, music, theatre and visual art. In this fictional example, I enjoy reading short stories because I can finish one in a single evening. I like discussing the characters and imagining what I would do in their situation. A story does not have to end happily to be interesting.

Films offer a different experience because the director chooses the images, sounds and pace. When a film is based on a book, some details may be changed or left out. I prefer judging each version on its own rather than expecting them to be identical. I also enjoy occasional visits to a gallery or a small concert. Culture can be shared with friends, but it can also be a quiet personal activity. For a student, libraries and local events can provide opportunities to discover something unfamiliar.

## Otázky a krátké vzorové odpovědi

1. **What do you notice when reading a story?**\
   I notice how the characters make decisions and how the narrator presents them.

2. **Would you recommend a book only because it is famous?**\
   No; I would explain who might enjoy it and why.

3. **Are film adaptations always faithful?**\
   No, because films use a different medium and have limited running time.

4. **What is special about live performances?**\
   The audience and performers share the same moment and atmosphere.

5. **How can people discover new culture?**\
   They can visit a library, attend a local event or ask friends for suggestions.

## Gramatika a časté chyby

O ději běžně mluvíme v **present simple**: The main character leaves home. **The book was written by...** je trpný rod. Rozliš **a character** (postava) a **an actor** (herec). **Interested in**, nikoli interested about. Vyjádři názor a důvod: I found it moving because...

## Obrázek a dialog: mini úkol

Vyber jednu přečtenou knihu: řekni autora, žánr, prostředí, konflikt a vlastní hodnocení v šesti větách. V dialogu vyber s kamarádem kulturní program; porovnej kino a divadlo a dohodni cenu i čas bez vymýšlení aktuálního programu.
