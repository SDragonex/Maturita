## Modelový projev B1/B2

*Originální fiktivní příklad pro jazykový nácvik. Osobní údaje a zkušenosti nejsou údaje o autorovi ani o tobě; při vlastní odpovědi je nahraď.*

Media influence what people know about the world and what they choose to discuss. We can get information from television, radio, newspapers, websites and social media. In this fictional example, I usually read news online, but I do not treat every popular post as a reliable report. A dramatic headline may leave out important details.

Before sharing a surprising claim, I check who published it, when it was written and what evidence it provides. I also look for reporting from other independent sources. An opinion article can be useful, but I need to recognise that it presents a viewpoint. Social media help people communicate quickly, yet they can also distract us and spread mistakes. I prefer to control notifications and think before publishing personal information. Media are valuable tools when we use them actively. Asking questions about a source is more useful than accepting or rejecting everything automatically.

## Otázky a krátké vzorové odpovědi

1. **How do you decide whether a source is reliable?**\
   I check its author, evidence, publication date and whether other sources confirm it.

2. **What is the difference between news and opinion?**\
   News reports events, while opinion explains somebody’s interpretation or judgement.

3. **Why can a headline be misleading?**\
   It may exaggerate one detail or omit information needed to understand the story.

4. **What do you like about social media?**\
   They make it easy to stay in touch and discover useful material.

5. **What should you consider before sharing a photo?**\
   I should consider privacy and whether the people in it agree.

## Gramatika a časté chyby

**News** je nepočitatelné a má jednotnou shodu: The news **is** surprising. **Information** bez -s. Nepřímá otázka má oznamovací slovosled: I want to know **who wrote it**. Rozliš **say something** a **tell someone something**.
