## Modelový projev B1/B2

*Originální fiktivní příklad pro jazykový nácvik. Osobní údaje a zkušenosti nejsou údaje o autorovi ani o tobě; při vlastní odpovědi je nahraď.*

There is no single best means of transport for every journey. A bicycle can be convenient for a short local trip, while a train may be a comfortable choice between cities. In this fictional example, I recently travelled to another town with a friend. We bought return tickets and checked the departure board at the station. Our train was delayed, so we informed the person who was meeting us.

During the journey, we could talk and look out of the window instead of concentrating on traffic. Travelling by car offers more flexibility, especially when a destination has poor public transport. However, parking and traffic jams can be a problem. Flying is useful for some long distances, but passengers also need time to reach the airport and complete the necessary procedures. When I plan a trip, I compare the whole journey, including transfers, luggage arrangements and the final route to my accommodation.

## Otázky a krátké vzorové odpovědi

1. **How do you usually travel short distances?**\
   I would choose walking when the route is convenient and the weather is suitable.

2. **What do you check before a train journey?**\
   I check the departure time, platform and whether I need to change trains.

3. **What would you do after missing a bus?**\
   I would find the next connection and tell anyone waiting for me.

4. **Why do some people prefer cars?**\
   A car allows them to choose their route and carry more things.

5. **What can be stressful about flying?**\
   Queues, delays and unfamiliar airport procedures can be stressful.

## Gramatika a časté chyby

Říkáme **by bus / by train**, ale **on foot**. Nastoupit: get on a bus; get into a car. **Travel** je obecně cestování, **a journey** jedna cesta, **a trip** výlet. Luggage je nepočitatelné: two pieces of luggage, nikoli two luggages.
