## Modelový projev B1/B2

*Originální fiktivní příklad pro jazykový nácvik. Osobní zkušenosti nejsou údaje o autorovi ani o tobě; uprav je pro vlastní odpověď.*

Technology helps people communicate, learn and solve practical problems. A smartphone combines several tools, such as a camera, a map and a communication device. Cloud services make it possible to access documents from different devices, but users should understand where their data is stored and who can access it.

In this fictional example, I use a laptop for programming and a phone for everyday communication. I try to choose tools according to the task instead of buying a device only because it is new. Artificial intelligence can help generate ideas or explain a concept. However, an answer may contain mistakes, so I need to check important information and test suggested code. I would not share passwords or confidential documents with an unfamiliar service.

New technology also raises questions about privacy, accessibility and electronic waste. A useful product should be understandable and reliable, not just impressive in a demonstration. In the future, I would like to develop an application that solves a clear problem for real users. I would start with a small version and improve it based on feedback.

## Otázky a krátké vzorové odpovědi

1. **How do you choose a device?**\
   I compare what I need with its cost and expected lifetime.

2. **Can AI always be trusted?**\
   No, important claims and code need checking.

3. **Why are backups useful?**\
   They help recover data after a failure or accidental deletion.

4. **What makes an application useful?**\
   It solves a clear problem and is easy to understand.
