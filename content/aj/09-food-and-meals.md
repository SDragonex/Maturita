## Modelový projev B1/B2

*Originální fiktivní příklad pro jazykový nácvik. Osobní údaje a zkušenosti nejsou údaje o autorovi ani o tobě; při vlastní odpovědi je nahraď.*

Food is connected with everyday routines, family traditions and meeting other people. In this fictional example, I usually have bread and fruit for breakfast. At lunchtime, I choose a meal at the school canteen. In the evening, I sometimes help prepare dinner at home. I enjoy simple dishes because I can learn to make them without complicated equipment.

One example is vegetable pasta. First, I prepare the vegetables and cook the pasta. Then I make a sauce and mix everything together. The exact ingredients depend on what is available. Eating in a restaurant can be a pleasant experience, but it usually costs more than cooking at home. When ordering, I ask politely and check any ingredients that matter to me. I also try to avoid wasting food by planning portions and using what we already have. My favourite meal is not necessarily expensive; the people around the table can make it special.

## Otázky a krátké vzorové odpovědi

1. **Do you enjoy cooking?**\
   Yes, especially simple meals that I can share with friends.

2. **What is the difference between a recipe and a menu?**\
   A recipe explains how to cook; a menu lists dishes you can order.

3. **How do you order politely?**\
   I would say: Could I have the vegetable soup, please?

4. **What would you ask about an unfamiliar dish?**\
   I would ask what ingredients it contains and how it is prepared.

5. **Why do people eat out?**\
   They may want convenience, a celebration or a dish they cannot make themselves.

## Gramatika a časté chyby

**Some / any**: We have some rice; do we have any rice? Rozliš **much rice** a **many apples**. Recept používá rozkaz: Chop the onions. **I would like** je zdvořilá objednávka; I like vyjadřuje obecnou oblibu. Food je obvykle nepočitatelné.
